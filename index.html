חסד של אמת

A kindness of truth

Caring for the deceased is called chesed shel emes, a kindness of truth, because it is given to one who can never repay it. The volunteers of the Chevra Kadisha perform this mitzvah with the utmost respect and dignity (kavod hames), as Jewish communities have for generations.

We offer burial preparation to any Jewish person in Greater Cleveland, regardless of denomination or observance, and we are available to guide families with sensitivity through any question about an imminent or recent death.

The Chevra Kadisha works alongside the funeral home. The funeral home handles transportation, paperwork and the service itself; the Chevra Kadisha performs the tahara and prepares the deceased for burial according to Jewish law.

## What to ask for

When you call the funeral home, tell them you want a traditional Jewish funeral. Specifically:

- **Tahara by the Chevra Kadisha:** the ritual washing and purification. [What is tahara?]({{ '/education/what-is-jewish-burial/' | relative_url }}#tahara-the-preparation)
- **Shmira:** someone to stay with the deceased until burial. [About shmira]({{ '/education/what-is-jewish-burial/' | relative_url }}#shmira-the-vigil)
- **Tachrichim:** simple white linen burial shrouds. [About tachrichim]({{ '/education/what-is-jewish-burial/' | relative_url }}#tachrichim-the-shroud)
- **A plain wooden aron (casket)** with no metal. [About the aron]({{ '/education/what-is-jewish-burial/' | relative_url }}#aron-the-casket)
- **In-ground burial** in a Jewish cemetery. [About kvura]({{ '/education/what-is-jewish-burial/' | relative_url }}#kvura-bikarka-in-ground-burial)

## What to avoid

Traditional Jewish practice does not include embalming, cosmetics, viewing of the body, or cremation. If you are unsure about anything the funeral home offers, please [ask us]({{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}) before agreeing to it.

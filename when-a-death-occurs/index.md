You do not need to know what to do. Call us first, before making other arrangements, and we will guide you through each step with care.

## First steps

1. **Call the Chevra Kadisha** at [{{ site.phone }}](tel:{{ site.phone_link }}). If you cannot reach us, see the [emergency contacts]({{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}#in-case-of-emergency).
2. **If the death occurred at home**, call the hospice nurse if hospice was involved, or the physician. If the death was unexpected, call 911.
3. **If the death occurred in a hospital or care facility**, tell the staff that the family wishes a traditional Jewish burial, that the Chevra Kadisha will be caring for the deceased, and that the family does not consent to an autopsy.
4. **Contact a funeral home** and tell them you want a traditional Jewish funeral, with tahara performed by the Chevra Kadisha. See [Working with Funeral Homes]({{ '/when-a-death-occurs/funeral-homes/' | relative_url }}).
5. **Notify your rabbi**, who can guide the family on the funeral and on the customs of mourning.

## At the time of death

It is a mitzvah to treat the deceased with dignity from the very first moments. If you can, stay with your loved one; reciting Tehillim (Psalms) is customary. Please do not move or wash the body; the Chevra Kadisha will guide you, as customs vary.

## Burial as soon as possible

Jewish tradition calls for burial as soon as possible, ideally within 24 hours. Delays should only be for the honor of the deceased, such as allowing close family to arrive. The Chevra Kadisha and the funeral home will help you coordinate the timing.

## Autopsy

Jewish law regards the body as sacred and generally opposes autopsy. If a coroner or medical examiner is involved, contact the Chevra Kadisha and your rabbi right away so we can help you make the family's wishes known.

## Helpful information to have ready

- The full English and Hebrew name of the deceased, and the Hebrew name of their father (and mother)
- The name of the cemetery and plot location, if one has been purchased
- Contact details for the closest relatives
- Any written wishes, such as a Halachic Living Will or [EMES card]({{ '/resources/emes-card/' | relative_url }})

Don't worry if you don't have all of this at hand. We can help you find it.

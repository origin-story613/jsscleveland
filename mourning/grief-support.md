Grief does not end when shiva ends. In the weeks and months that follow, many people find comfort in talking with others, whether a rabbi, a counselor, or a support group.

## Where to find support

- **Your rabbi** is often the best first call, for spiritual guidance and for a referral to local support.
- **Your physician** can refer you to a grief counselor if you are struggling.
- **The Chevra Kadisha** can point you toward resources in the community. [Contact us]({{ '/contact/' | relative_url }})

*We are putting together a list of local Cleveland support groups, counselors and bikur cholim organizations. If you know of a resource that should be listed here, please [let us know]({{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}).*

## If you need help now

If you or someone you know is in crisis, call or text **988** (the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) at any hour, or call 911.
